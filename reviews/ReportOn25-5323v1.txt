This manuscript represents a revised submission of an article focused
on monocular SLAM-based navigation for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).
Following the initial review stage, two experts in the field provided
feedback on the original version of this article. Both reviewers agree
that the authors have adequately addressed their comments, supplying
additional relevant materials and experimental results, making the
paper more convincing.

I concur with the reviewers that the manuscript still requires a
discussion part to position the results and describe the limitations of
the proposed method. Therefore, I recommend revising and resubmitting
the article to address this shortcoming.
