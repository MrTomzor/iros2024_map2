I thank the authors for addressing the points I raised in my prior
review of this paper. In my view, the authors have revised the paper in
an appropriate manner. The contribution relative to the closely related
literature is now better explained. Several aspects of the proposed
method are now more clearly presented both in the text and in the
figures. In addition, the experimental results are also more complete
and convincing in the revised version.

A minor comment is that if MonoSpheres is the name of the proposed
approach, it could be a good idea to have this name appear in the
abstract and the introduction, perhaps in other places too. Right now,
it appears in the title and then it does not appear again until page 6.
